Understanding the brief

Context

ICICI approached our team because they needed to know why their users use other UPI platforms instead of the bank app UPI, and how to bring traction to their UPI.

As of 2022, 81% of the UPI market had Google Pay, PhonePe and PayTM users. 71% of ICICI customers didn’t do UPI payments from the bank app, but used it for other tasks.

So we conducted an in-depth research to solve this issue.

The Objective

To curate a research handbook that addressed the following challenges:

01
No first-mover advantage

How do you make ICICI UPI stand out without a first mover advantage?

02
Reliability

How do we make ICICI UPI be so reliable that users deem it more reliable than their current UPI platforms?

03
What to test

How and what scenarios do we test to identify the correct problems and insights?

04
Where next

What design directions can ICICI take after this research?

The Research Plan

The research was structured from a broad to a narrow spectrum using a funnel method. 24 users across 5 tier-1 cities in India were interviewed.

Five scenarios, tested

We prototyped five real-world UPI tasks and watched users attempt each one in iMobile Pay, with no hints.

Scenario 01

Scan & Pay at Subway

We asked users to complete a payment at Subway using iMobile Pay’s Scan & Pay flow — one of the most common real-world UPI tasks. No hints.

8–10sAvg. time just to locate the UPI entry point
3/5Users tapped the wrong entry point first
2×Avg. extra navigations before reaching Scan & Pay
0/5Users who completed the task without hesitation

Scenario 02

Remote QR Payment

Users were asked to pay a merchant via a QR code sent over WhatsApp — without being in the same room.

Scenario 03

Pay to Contact

Users were asked to send money directly to a saved phone contact using iMobile Pay.

Scenario 04

Repeat a Recent Transaction

Users were asked to repeat a payment they had made previously using the transaction history.

Scenario 05

Third-Party App Payment

Users were asked to complete a payment initiated from a third-party app via UPI deep-link.

Design challenge #1

No first-mover advantage

We had to address the “hard truth” that ICICI bank has no first-mover advantage against its competitors. So, to stand out as a UPI platform, they would have to innovate on the branding, marketing and offers cohorts.

Design challenge #2

Security concerns

The environment impacts the user’s decision to open the right app.

Users resort to Gpay / Phonepe / PayTM for small payments because:

Privacy Concerns Users assume Gpay/Phonepe is more prone to fraud Bank apps are better for bigger payments Payments take lesser time
“They can see my balance also, so I dont open the app in public.”
“I wont open my banking app around many people they will see my password and snatch my phone.”

Design challenge #3

Labels

Users take more time to complete UPI payments on the app due to confusing labels.
