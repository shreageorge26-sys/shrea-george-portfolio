06Scenarios

Where people got stuck, task by task.

        07Finding 1 of 4

        No first-mover advantage

        Everyone already does UPI.

        ICICI arrived late. To stand out, it had to compete on branding, marketing and offers.

        08Finding 2 of 4

        Context decides the app

        Where you’re standing decides which app you open.

        Big payments at home went through the banking app. Small payments, wherever people were, went to a UPI app.

        Public spaces
        Big paymentCredit card
        Small paymentUPI app
        Big paymentBanking app
        Small paymentUPI app
        Controlled environments
        Why small payments go to GPay, PhonePe or Paytm Privacy in public They feel faster Bank apps feel right for bigger amounts UPI apps seem riskier, so they only get small amounts
        09Finding 3 of 4

        Security, in public

        Nobody opens their bank app in a crowd.

        They can see my balance also, so I don’t open the app in public.User interview
        I won’t open my banking app around many people. They will see my password and snatch my phone.User interview

        And when a payment gets stuck, the bank gets blamed.

        10Finding 4 of 4

        Labels

        Labels that sound alike slow everyone down.

        “Send money”, “BHIM UPI”, “Pay to contact”: people couldn’t tell which one would do what they wanted, so every payment took longer.
